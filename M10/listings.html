Listings in Quarto

What Is a Listing?

A Quarto Listing is a structured collection of items displayed as a table, grid, or custom list. Listings are useful for projects, blog posts, reports, or portfolio pieces.

Example Configuration

listing: contents: projects; type: grid; categories: true; sort: date desc
